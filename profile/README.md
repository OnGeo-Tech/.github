# OnGeo

### Geospatial technology · GIS · Location intelligence

From Earth observation to actionable geospatial intelligence.

OnGeo develops geospatial technologies that connect Earth observation, GIS and location intelligence. We integrate satellite imagery, aerial observations and spatial datasets to support analysis of land, buildings, infrastructure and the environment.

Our approach combines global geospatial expertise with local data and regional context, transforming complex geographic information into practical insights for informed decisions.

## What we do

- **GIS & spatial analysis** — connect geographic datasets to uncover patterns and relationships.
- **Earth observation & remote sensing** — use satellite and aerial imagery to analyze locations, assess terrain and monitor changes over time.
- **Location intelligence** — make information about places easier to explore, understand and use.
- **Property & land insights** — transform maps, public records and terrain information into practical reports.
- **Multi-source data integration** — combine satellite and aerial imagery, LiDAR, terrain models, vector maps, cadastral information and environmental datasets.

## Precision data acquisition

We collaborate with [AlterGeo Holding](https://altergeoholding.com/) to acquire precise geospatial data for our analyses. AlterGeo's aerial sensing technologies combine photogrammetric imaging and LiDAR capabilities, complementing satellite observations with detailed information about terrain and the built environment.

By connecting data acquisition with multi-source spatial analysis, we turn observations into meaningful location intelligence.
