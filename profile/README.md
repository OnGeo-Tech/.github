# OnGeo

### Geospatial technology · GIS · Location intelligence

We turn complex spatial data into clear, actionable insights.

OnGeo combines GIS expertise, geospatial technology and property data analysis to help people understand places and make informed decisions about land, buildings and their surroundings. We bring global geospatial expertise together with local data and regional context.

## What we do

- **GIS & spatial analysis** — connect geographic datasets to uncover patterns and relationships.
- **Location intelligence** — make information about places easier to explore, understand and use.
- **Property & land insights** — transform maps, public records and terrain information into practical reports.
- **Multi-source data integration** — combine satellite and aerial imagery, LiDAR, terrain models, vector maps, cadastral information and environmental datasets.
